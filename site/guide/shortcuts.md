# Keyboard shortcuts

Every shortcut in Cutaway’s menus. Type in the field to find one by what it does, by its menu, or by its keys, such as “shift cmd e”.

<Shortcuts />

## In the preview

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Select a layer | Click it |
| Expand or collapse a layer’s row | Double-click the layer |
| Turn the layers (3D) | Drag |
| Pan | Scroll; in 2D, you can also drag |
| Zoom around the pointer | Pinch, or <kbd>⌘</kbd>-scroll |
| Measure from the selected layer | Hold <kbd>⌥</kbd> and point at another layer |

## In the layer list

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Expand or collapse a row | Double-click it |
| Expand or collapse a row with everything inside | <kbd>⌥</kbd>-click its disclosure triangle |
| Measure from the selected layer | Hold <kbd>⌥</kbd> and point at another row |

## In the attributes

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Fold or unfold every section | <kbd>⌥</kbd>-click a section header |
| Put back a value the app would refuse | <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or leave the field |

## In the app list

Select an app with the arrow keys and inspect it with <kbd>Return</kbd>.
